# Office Hours diagnostic

**Mode:** startup, pre-product. **Status:** evidence-constrained recommendation.

## Specific customer
The initial buyer is the founder/operations lead of a 2–10 person US packaged-food brand preparing its first or next regional grocery placement. Their reputation and cash conversion depend on delivering buyer-ready SKU data without delaying launch.

## Painful status quo
The founder coordinates label artwork, formulas, UPC ownership, lab certificates, case specs, and buyer forms across email, spreadsheets, vendors, and memory. A missing certificate or mismatched field appears late, forcing rework or risking a buyer-timeline reset. The source names the workflow but provides no observed time or loss data; that is the central gap.

## Desperate use case and narrowest paid wedge
Do not build a generic “food brand agent.” Sell one **retail readiness sprint** for one SKU and one retailer: inventory evidence, name blockers, assign next actions, and produce a founder-approved packet. Test **$499 per SKU** as a concierge hypothesis. It is below the source’s reported $1,999 roadmap offer, but no payment evidence exists.

## Observation and validation
Watch five founders prepare a real buyer packet without coaching. Record tabs, handoffs, corrections, missing artifacts, elapsed time, and restarts. Offer ten qualified founders a $499 sprint with a refundable deposit. Success: 3 paid sprints, median packet time under 90 minutes, and 2 founders reusing it for a second SKU. Kill below 2 deposits or if the pain is mainly expert advice software cannot safely standardize.

## Future-fit
Retailer portals and compliance data will become more structured, making reliable cross-retailer evidence mapping more valuable. Generic extraction gets cheaper; the defensible layer is verified mappings, provenance, expiry, and accepted outcomes. If retailers standardize end-to-end, a connector becomes less essential, so own exception handling rather than form filling.

## Assignment
Recruit five shelf-visible local brands and observe one real onboarding session for each before adding automation.
